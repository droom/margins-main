MARGINS is the independent design practice of Chris Droom. As it develops, I hope this to become a co-operative group of like-minded practitioners. If you have skills in illustration, lettering, or 3d-modelling and would like to collaborate, please get in touch. Living as we did—on the edge—we developed a particular way of seeing reality. We looked both from the outside in and from the inside out. This mode of seeing reminded us of the existence of a whole universe, a main body made up of both margin and center.
